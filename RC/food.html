Food and Recipes

Cooking is relaxing, and there is food to enjoy at the end of it. I like to meal prep and cook food for around 3 days so I can enjoy quick meals without having to cook different food every day. My two favourites are peking style beef mince, chicken wraps, and honey soy salmon (which I will describe the recipe for).

Honey Soy Salmon

Ingredients

  • Salmon Fillets
  • Dark Soy Sauce
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Seasonings: salt, pepper, onion powder, paprika
  • Honey
  • Rice vinegar
  • Rice
  • Brocolli (or any other vegetable)

You can find all of these ingredients at all major supermarkets such as Asda, Tesco, and Morrisons.

Method

  1. Add the salmon to a bowl. Add the soy sauce, garlic, ginger, seasonings, honey and rice vinegar.
  2. Mix so the salmon is evenly coated. Leave it to marinate for as long as you wish.
  3. Add the salmon to a frying pan with some oil and fry until cooked throughout.
  4. Plate the salmon with rice and vegetables of choice
  5. Optionally top with sesame seeds
Positives Negatives
Quick to make Salmon is expensive
Easy to meal prep The rice can be bland
