Honey Soy Salmon

Ingredients

  • Salmon Fillets
  • Dark Soy Sauce
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Seasonings: salt, pepper, onion powder, paprika
  • Honey
  • Rice vinegar
  • Rice
  • Brocolli (or any other vegetable)

Method

  1. Add the salmon to a bowl. Add the soy sauce, garlic, ginger seasonings, honey and rice vinegar.
  2. Mix so the salmon is evenly coated. Leave it to marinate for as long as you wish.
  3. Add the salmon to a frying pan with some oil and fry until cooked throughout.
  4. Plate the salmon with rice and vegetables of choice
  5. Optionally top with sesame seeds
